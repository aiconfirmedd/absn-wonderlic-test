# Wonderholic ABSN Guide research

Research date: 2026-08-20

## Source hierarchy

1. Official Wonderlic instructions control test format and test-day behavior.
2. Established test-preparation publishers are used for recognition and practice methods.
3. Reddit is treated as experience-based corroboration, never policy.

## Best free video starting points

- [JobTestPrep — Wonderlic Test: Learn How to Ace This Cognitive Test](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ordmd9PZB_U): strongest format overview; covers verbal, numerical, spatial, and speed questions, repeated practice, and full simulations.
- [Online Training for Everyone — Pass the Wonderlic Assessment Test](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LAW2bOqcH5w): recent worked-question walkthrough emphasizing type recognition and speed.

## Verified operating strategy

The [official Wonderlic sample](https://wonderlic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/07.11.24-Select-Sample-Assessment-Downloadable.pdf) states that the cognitive portion has 50 questions in 12 minutes, disallows calculators and dictionaries, says many people do not finish, directs candidates not to skip around or spend too long on one item, and recommends marking a best choice when reasonably unsure.

Community reports consistently emphasize recognizing personally slow categories, eliminating implausible choices, and avoiding overthinking. One detailed [r/jobs experience](https://www.reddit.com/r/jobs/comments/13ml0ha/wonscorewonderlic_test/) specifically recommends using answer choices to simplify apparently complex math. These are useful practice observations, not official scoring rules.

## Best method by question family

- Quick recognition: dates use year → month → day; duplicates use character-by-character comparison; alphabetical items stop at the first differing character.
- Verbal: define each word briefly; for analogies, state the relationship before viewing choices; for odd-one-out, find the category shared by four.
- Math: read the requested quantity first, normalize units, identify the base, and estimate against the choices before exact arithmetic.
- Number series: test ratio across two gaps, then first differences, then alternating rules; verify the rule more than once.
- Logic: diagram only the stated premises; separate contradicted (false) from unsupported (not certain); never reverse “if A, then B.”
- Spatial: track one feature; mirrors preserve height while swapping left/right; rotations move both location and orientation; odd-one-out depends on handedness.
- General knowledge: answer from immediate recall or protect the clock with the best supported choice.

## Memorization and mindset

- Use a compact cue for each category instead of memorizing long prose.
- Practice in the actual 12-minute constraint after untimed method review.
- Review every miss and every correct answer that took over 25 seconds.
- Treat the task as collecting correct answers, not proving that every problem can be solved.
