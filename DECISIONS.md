# Decisions

- Keep all 50 verified Form 01 questions unchanged.
- Separate study mode from timed exam mode so teaching material does not distract during the test.
- Use a restrained academic navy, paper, and gold visual system without implying university affiliation.
- Preserve Supabase result submission and its public-client insert-only access model.
- Require a first name before the quiz starts; detailed result upload remains optional and consent-gated.
- Provide a separate Explore Mode with free previous/next navigation, visible answers and tips, and no timer, score, saved attempt, name requirement, or upload.
- Require an explicit Next action after tap/key selection so answers can be reviewed before they are logged.
- Keep official guidance distinct from community tactics and avoid unsupported universal school-score claims.
