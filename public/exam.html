Review rules and score strategy

Practice rules

  • Timer starts when you click Start. Auto-submits at 0:00.
  • One question at a time. You must answer to advance — no skipping, no going back in this simulator.
  • No calculator. Keep scratch paper + pencil next to you.
  • This practice score counts one point per correct answer and does not subtract for errors.
  • Select with a tap or keys 1–5 / A–E, then press Next to log the answer.

The 4 rules that raise your score

  • 20-second rule: if a question will take more than ~20s, pick the most plausible answer and move. A hard question is worth the same 1 point as an easy one.
  • First 25 are your points: early questions are easiest. Bank them fast and accurately — target under 10s each.
  • Read the question stem first on word problems; know what's asked before parsing numbers.
  • Choose with elimination: remove obviously wrong options, then commit to the best supported answer.
